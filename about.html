About

01 Origin

MASK Capital Management is being developed as a research-led proprietary trading identity focused on quantitative methods and disciplined capital deployment.

Our work brings together derivatives analysis, systematic strategy development, execution engineering and risk oversight.

We believe a robust trading practice requires more than an attractive backtest. It demands a repeatable research process, thoughtful position sizing, live monitoring and the willingness to reassess assumptions when market conditions change.

02 Mission

To develop systematic trading capabilities grounded in evidence, operational reliability and risk awareness.

03 Vision

To build a durable, adaptable quantitative research and trading organisation.

04 Operating Principles

What shapes every decision.

  1. Research before conviction

    Hypotheses are tested, monitored and revised.

  2. Risk before return

    Exposures, drawdowns and operational safeguards shape decisions.

  3. Systems over impulse

    Reproducible processes, automation and measured execution.

  4. Adaptability over certainty

    Strategies are evaluated across changing market regimes.

05 Research Culture

Evidence over narrative.

Our work sits at the intersection of markets, mathematics and engineering. We value curiosity, clear accountability, thoughtful debate and a disciplined approach to execution.
