About

Behind every great company is a long view.

We are a focused team of investors and operators. We back a small number of businesses each year and commit the time, capital and network it takes to help them compound.

Our name reflects how we work: discreet, disciplined and in service of the companies we support. The spotlight belongs to our founders. Our job is to make their next chapter easier to write.
